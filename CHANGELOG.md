# Changelog

## v1.0.0

First release for World of Warcraft: Forever.

- Match-3 board that opens from the minimap button, the addon compartment or `/gemcutter`.
- Endless play: the board reshuffles when no moves are left and is saved between sessions.
- Special gems: line gems from four in a row, volatile gems from L and T shapes, prisms from five
  in a row.
- Account-wide progression: levels 1 to 60, a rank title every ten levels, 18 achievements and a
  stats panel.
- Drag gems or click two neighbours to swap. Gems follow the cursor while you drag.
- Minimap button built on LibDBIcon, so minimap button collectors and broker bars can manage it.
- The window closes on combat and ready checks.
